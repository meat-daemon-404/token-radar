How the ranking works

implied = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × chains × category × breakout

Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −60%…+120%. Maturity is how long the protocol has run with no token (0.6–1.0). Chains rewards a multi-chain footprint (up to 1.20). Category gives 1.10 to fee-earning categories (Dexs, Lending, Derivatives, Yield) and 0.90 to RWA and stablecoins. Breakout (1.10) marks a protocol younger than 180 days growing faster than 20% in a week. No protocol name is special-cased: the ranking follows only from the snapshot's own figures.

A protocol counts as a launch candidate only when it records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades — the same rule the watchlist itself uses. Wrapped Bitcoin, staked ETH and bridge tokens are left out: none of them is a protocol still waiting on a launch of its own.

Implied value is this radar's own claim, never a promise. A protocol is said to record no token, never to have none: the snapshot is DefiLlama's record, not proof. Farming costs gas and time, and an airdrop may never come.
